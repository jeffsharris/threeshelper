## Mandatory MDE And Power Preflight

No S3 policy outcome may run until this preflight is complete and sealed.

### Inputs

Use only historical control-arm annotations and outcome-free corpus metadata to
estimate:

- milestone base rate by primary stratum;
- root-level heterogeneity;
- within-root repeat correlation;
- terminal rate;
- available fresh-root count by family and stratum;
- expected treatment runtime from existing C1 measurements.

Do not inspect or generate any S3 treatment outcome.

### Candidate Designs

Evaluate candidate designs using:

- roots: `96`, `128`, `160`, and `192`;
- repeats per arm/root: `4`, `6`, and `8`;
- two deterministic stream blocks with equal or near-equal repeat counts.

Prefer more independent roots over more repeats whenever two designs have
similar power. Choose exactly one design before outcomes.

### Power Method

- Simulate paired clustered Bernoulli outcomes under the observed stratum base
  rates and root heterogeneity.
- Use at least `10,000` deterministic simulation draws per candidate design.
- Apply the exact planned strata-standardized estimator and
  ancestry-clustered interval.
- Report power at common odds ratios `1.25`, `1.50`, and `2.00`.
- Report the 80%-power minimum detectable common odds ratio and the
  corresponding stratum-specific absolute percentage-point effects.
- Report expected wall time and storage.

### Coherence Rule

Freeze the smallest feasible, root-preferred design with:

- at least `80%` power for a common odds ratio of `1.50`;
- at least `48` roots in each primary stratum;
- at least three families and the `40%` family cap;
- expected storage below `10 GiB`;
- enough free disk to remain above `100 GiB`.

If no candidate design is coherent, stop as `HOLD_UNDERPOWERED_PREFLIGHT`.
Increase root diversity or redesign the estimator without outcomes. Do not run
an underpowered S3 and do not convert the result into a kill.

## Primary Estimand

For each primary stratum, define:

- `pre1536`: reach the first non-starter `1536` by h40;
- `pre3072`: reach the first non-starter `3072` by h40.

Within each arm and stratum, weight roots equally. Let the stratum log-odds
effect be the treatment-minus-control logit difference in root-equal milestone
probability, using a fixed Jeffreys correction only when needed for a boundary
rate.

The primary effect is the equal-weight average of the two stratum log-odds
effects. Report it as a common odds ratio. Infer uncertainty by bootstrapping
whole ancestries, preserving all repeats and both arms within a sampled root.

This estimator and its equal stratum weights are frozen before outcomes. Do not
replace it with whichever absolute, relative, or pooled scale looks strongest.

## Three-Way S3 Decision

Let `OR` be the frozen common odds-ratio estimate and `CI` its ancestry-cluster
95% interval.

### `PASS_SCIENTIFIC_UTILITY`

Require all:

- `OR >= 1.25`;
- lower 95% confidence bound `> 1.00`;
- no primary stratum has a clearly negative milestone effect;
- h40 score is not materially inferior, using a frozen `-5%` relative
  non-inferiority margin;
- survival is no worse than `-2 pp`;
- anchor preservation is no worse than `-3 pp`;
- no concentrated catastrophic family or ancestry failure;
- exactness and stream audits pass.

This promotes only the scientific hypothesis. It does not promote a policy.
C2 becomes eligible as the engineering bridge to deployable selective depth 3.

### `FAIL_NO_MEANINGFUL_UTILITY`

Require all:

- upper 95% confidence bound `< 1.25`, so the experiment excludes the frozen
  minimum meaningful effect;
- no audit or power failure;
- no major stratum is too sparse for the frozen estimator.

This kills S3 and C2 while preserving G1 as the next self-learning branch.

### `HOLD_INCONCLUSIVE`

Any result between the pass and fail conditions is inconclusive. Record the
interval, power, and limiting source. Do not call it a permanent kill and do
not rerun the same design. The next step must add independent roots, improve
the estimator without outcome-driven tuning, or proceed to G1.
