# G1 Relational Action-Hazard Execution Charter

Date frozen: 2026-07-25

Status: authoritative preregistration; no G1 rollout label or model outcome may
exist before the outcome-free preflight below is sealed `READY`.

## Scientific Question

Can explicit relational afterstate features predict and select legal actions
that improve the stage-appropriate h40 next-milestone hazard beyond both:

1. a parameter-budget-matched positional/cell-feature logistic model; and
2. the frozen incumbent depth-2 action?

The two strata and direct endpoints are fixed:

- `pre1536`: root built maximum excluding the starter is exactly `768`; target
  is first non-starter `1536` by h40.
- `pre3072`: root built maximum excluding the starter is exactly `1536`;
  target is first non-starter `3072` by h40.

H10 and h20 are secondary readouts from the same h40 path. Human choices are
never labels. This is not a scalar geometry bonus, n-tuple sidecar, context
residual, imitation model, planner, or search-depth experiment.

## G1 Corpus Contract

The corpus is natural, simulator-valid, and selected before labels.

### Exclusions

Exclude every whole ancestry in:

- incumbent parent/component training sources;
- R1/R1b/A2 inventories and evaluations;
- R2a and C1 inventories;
- both the S3 exclusion union and every S3 surviving candidate root;
- every prior selector, reachability, frontier, first-action, continuation,
  MCTS/UCT, support, transition, human, or assisted-human outcome gate.

Aliases and replay copies are deduplicated by canonical fresh root ancestry,
not path. S3-excluded or S3-surviving roots may not be borrowed to fill G1.

### State Selection

- Select at most one state per ancestry.
- A candidate state must restore exact board, score, preview, tile-cycle/deck
  counters, pending state, starter, move count, and legal actions.
- State selection is deterministic from source/root/state hashes after
  stratifying by milestone rung and source success/control role.
- `source_success_window` means the source replay naturally reaches the direct
  milestone within the next 40 recorded moves. `source_control` means it does
  not. This historical role is used only to preserve coverage; it is never a
  feature, target action, or sample weight.
- Partition whole roots before labels with namespaces
  `G1-train-v1`, `G1-validation-v1`, and `G1-test-v1`. No root, source alias, or
  root seed may cross partitions.

### Readiness

All must pass before labels:

- at least five genuine nonhuman behavior families overall;
- no family above `40%` of roots in any ordinary partition;
- train at least `256` roots, validation at least `96`;
- train, validation, and untouched test each contain both milestone strata and
  both historical source roles, with at least `10` roots in every
  stratum-role cell;
- untouched-test size is the smallest frozen design in
  `{512,768,1024,1536,2048,3072}` roots at `8` repeats per legal action that
  attains at least `80%` conservative ancestry-cluster power for a common OR
  `1.50` when only a preregistered `30%` of roots are active disagreements and
  the remaining `70%` are exact structural-zero same-action pairs. OR `1.50`
  is defined for the strata-standardized policy effect over all roots; the
  simulator solves the larger implied active-root OR needed after dilution;
- test has at least three families and at least `48` roots per stratum;
- exact state/provenance failures, prior-root overlaps, cross-partition root
  overlaps, and stream collisions are all zero;
- measured free disk exceeds `100 GiB`.

The conservative design-power model uses historical control annotations only:
beta-binomial root heterogeneity, equal stratum weights, conditionally
independent active-arm residuals given latent root propensity, exact
structural-zero inactive roots, 10,000 simulations, and a whole-root cluster
influence standard error. The first design reaching 80% receives a
1,000-simulation sensitivity check with 199 whole-ancestry bootstraps before it
can be selected. Shared-uniform active-arm coupling is sensitivity only.
Both the lower-bound rejection rate and the complete frozen PASS event
(`point OR >=1.25` plus lower bound above one) must reach 80%.
The simulated median common OR must reproduce `1.50` within absolute log-OR
error `0.02`; a design failing this calibration check is invalid regardless of
nominal power.

If readiness fails, decision is `HOLD_G1_DATA_PREFLIGHT`. No labels or fitting
may run. The next artifact must be a genuinely fresh natural-root generation
plan, not weaker exclusions, extra repeats in place of roots, or recycled
evidence.

## Frozen Label Contract

Only a `READY` preflight authorizes labels.

- Evaluate every legal first action at each root.
- Force the first action, then continue with the frozen incumbent depth-2
  policy through h40.
- Use exactly `8` replicates per root/action, split into descriptive blocks
  `A=4` and `B=4`.
- One h40 trajectory supplies h10, h20, and h40 readouts.
- Legal actions on one root share deck stream IDs and slot uniform streams;
  each trajectory maps the shared slot uniform over its own legal insertion
  positions. Policy/tie streams are independent and frozen.
- G1 stream namespaces must be disjoint from every prior manifest.
- Store compact horizon sufficient statistics and at most 24 fixed replay
  audits, never all trajectories.
- Primary label is the empirical h40 direct-milestone probability for each
  root/action. Secondary outcomes are h10/h20 hazard, h40 score gain, survival,
  anchor integrity, empties, and the frozen relational geometry readouts.
- Resume, deterministic replay, exact context, terminal censoring, missing
  action-cell, and stream collision audits must pass before fitting.

No partial outcome peeking, label-driven repartition, root removal, repeat
extension, or state replacement is permitted.

Train and validation labels are generated first. Untouched-test all-action
labels do not exist yet. After both models are serialized, the untouched test
features may be scored outcome-blind to freeze model actions and run the
activity/power checkpoint below. Only a passing activity checkpoint authorizes
one separate untouched-test label job. If test tasks must be pre-materialized
for operational reasons, their outcome files remain access-blocked and
unreadable until the model, selection, activity, and hash lock is complete.

## Frozen Models and Optimization

- Two binomial logistic regressions only: relational and positional.
- Each has `65` fitted parameters including intercept.
- The deterministic corpus selector enforces `n_family <= floor(0.40*N)` in
  every partition before labels. It orders roots by
  `SHA256("G1-family-cap-v1"|partition|family|root)` and retains the first
  admissible roots while satisfying frozen stratum-role cells. It never
  relabels policy aliases or duplicates minority roots.
- Every retained training ancestry has total weight `1/N_train`, independent
  of legal-action count. If root `r` has `L_r` legal actions, each action row
  has weight `1/(N_train*L_r)`. With `k` successes in exactly `8` trials, its
  contribution is that row weight times
  `-[k*log(p)+(8-k)*log(1-p)]/8`. Root-balanced metrics use the same
  `1/N_partition/L_r` action-row weights. Family-balanced metrics are reported
  secondarily with row weight `1/F/n_family/L_r`.
- Continuous inputs use train-only weighted mean and standard deviation;
  constant columns remain zero after masking.
- Coefficients and intercept initialize to zero.
- Deterministic full-batch Adam: seed `20260725`, learning rate `0.02`,
  `1,500` steps, L2 coefficient `0.01` on non-intercept coefficients, no early
  stopping.
- Validation performs only intercept recalibration by deterministic Newton
  updates with frozen coefficients. It cannot select features, regularization,
  epochs, seeds, or architecture.
- Save/load must preserve schema/version, normalization, coefficients,
  intercept, calibration intercept, and source hashes; incompatible schemas
  fail closed.
- One untouched test opening after both final serialized models are frozen.

## Outcome-Blind Test Activity Gate

After train/validation labels, final model serialization, and validation
intercept calibration, score untouched-test features without reading or
generating test outcomes. Freeze each relational-selected action, the frozen
incumbent action, and disagreement status by root/stratum/family.

Recompute the historical-control-only OR `1.50` power with:

- the observed disagreement roots active under the relational selector;
- same-action roots coupled as exact structural zeroes;
- the observed stratum/family activity pattern;
- eight repeats, beta-binomial root heterogeneity, 10,000 simulations, and 199
  ancestry bootstraps.

Require at least `20` disagreements across at least three families and at least
`80%` conservative power. Otherwise decision is
`HOLD_G1_INACTIVE_UNDERPOWERED`; untouched-test labels remain nonexistent or
sealed and unread. This gate cannot change the model, threshold, roots, or
actions.

## Untouched Offline Gate

All inference is root-equal and clustered by whole ancestry. Root-balanced and
family-balanced metrics are both reported.

### Predictive Requirements

All must pass:

- test improvement `(positional - relational)` in both Brier score and log loss
  has a 95% ancestry-bootstrap lower bound above zero under root balancing;
- both improvements have the same positive direction under family balancing
  and on every stratum; no one family supplies more than 50% of total gain;
- relational calibration has ECE `<=0.05`, slope in `[0.75,1.25]`, and
  absolute logit intercept `<=0.10`;
- relational milestone calibration is not worse than positional by more than
  `0.01` Brier or `0.02` ECE;
- empirical-vs-predicted within-root action-rank correlation is positive with
  ancestry-bootstrap lower bound above zero;
- relational and incumbent actions disagree on at least `20` untouched roots
  across at least three families.

Synthetic context swaps and human states may be reported separately but cannot
satisfy any gate.

### Causal Action-Selection Requirement

Use the already-labeled all-action test outcomes to compare the frozen
relational-selected action with the frozen incumbent action. Equal-weight the
two milestone strata and report the common odds ratio, absolute effects, and
whole-ancestry bootstrap interval. Stream-block signs are descriptive, not a
conjunction gate.

`PASS_G1_OFFLINE` requires:

- common OR point estimate `>=1.25` and 95% lower bound `>1.0`;
- neither stratum has point OR below `1.0`;
- h40 score lower bound is no worse than `-5%` of the incumbent h40 mean gain;
- survival difference is at least `-2 pp`;
- anchor-preservation difference is at least `-3 pp`;
- leave-one-family-out common OR remains above `1.0`;
- every predictive, integrity, and concentration requirement above passes.

`FAIL_G1_NO_ACTIONABLE_SIGNAL` applies when the untouched design had at least
80% power for OR `1.50` and either the common-OR upper bound is below `1.25`,
the relational model fails both primary predictive losses, or a no-harm bound
is violated. Otherwise the decision is `HOLD_G1_INCONCLUSIVE`. Neither permits
a sweep or reuse of the untouched test.

## Conditional Policy Path

A G1 offline pass authorizes exactly one calibrated low-margin reranker:

- only roots where relational probability exceeds incumbent by at least
  `0.10` and the incumbent normalized top-two margin is at most `2%`;
- residual probability is never converted into a hand-written score bonus;
- one fresh ancestry-disjoint all-action first-move causal gate precedes any
  normal-start evaluation.

A first-action pass may open one fresh paired normal-start development block.
Only a preregistered development pass may open one sealed confirmation.
Confirmation alone may update the incumbent or dashboard.

## Operational Decision Rules

- `READY_G1_PREFLIGHT`: run the frozen label job, then exactly two fits and one
  untouched offline gate without routine reauthorization.
- `HOLD_G1_DATA_PREFLIGHT`: generate no outcomes; preserve the audit and write
  a fresh natural-root generation plan.
- Engineering failures may be repaired without changing this scientific
  contract.
- Any scientific gate failure permanently closes this exact G1 formulation.
- No goal-conditioned planner is eligible unless relational prediction both
  generalizes and is causally actionable.
