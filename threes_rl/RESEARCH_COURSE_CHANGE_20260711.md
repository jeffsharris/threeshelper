# Threes Research Course-Change Recommendation

Date: 2026-07-11. Status: both preauthorized overnight branches exhausted;
no incumbent or dashboard change.

## What The Program Established

1. Exact context is causally material to depth-2 choices and continuations, but
   the frozen small board+context residual did not add source-disjoint value.
   It worsened ancestry-heldout MAE under both natural and family-balanced
   reporting and predicted synthetic context contrasts in the wrong rank
   direction. More fitting of this exact representation is not justified.
2. Adaptive depth 3 is behaviorally active. It changed 23 of 64 congested
   heldout decisions across corner2, legacy learned, and incumbent lineages.
   The blocker is computational: median 10.0x and p90 16.4x depth-2 runtime,
   versus frozen 3x/5x limits. The 2,048-node cap never bound, so budget tuning
   is not the missing ingredient; the depth-3 operator itself needs a cheaper
   implementation.
3. Assisted play is producing useful query evidence but not yet enough
   independent ancestry support. Two substantial completed games contain 34
   high-margin human/incumbent disagreements across two ancestries. This is
   below the frozen three-ancestry review threshold and is not action-label
   evidence.

## Recommended Next Program

Highest-probability next step is a two-part evidence acquisition program, not
another model or search hyperparameter sweep.

### 1. Complete Assisted-Disagreement Diversity

Acquire at least one additional substantial assisted game, preferably reaching
built 768. Once three independent substantial ancestries exist, prepare the
already-authorized compact review of high-margin disagreements. Select only
3-5 decorrelated, high-information roots and test every legal first action with
paired shared-deck h10/h20/h40 continuations. Human choice remains a query, not
a label. Continue only if direct score/milestone advantage replicates across
ancestries without survival or anchor harm.

### 2. Make Depth 3 Cheap Before Retesting It

Run an engineering-only search profile and microbenchmark, with no policy
outcomes, aimed at bringing the same R2a depth-3 calculation below 3x depth-2:

- reuse depth-2 transposition/action/afterstate work during iterative deepening
  instead of constructing independent shallow and deep searches;
- retain transposition entries across adjacent real-game decisions with exact
  state keys and bounded memory;
- batch or memoize chance-layer preview/slot expansions;
- order actions from depth-2 values and share a fair per-action node budget;
- report cache hit rate, unique expanded states, chance expansions, and time by
  layer before proposing another policy gate.

Do not rerun R2a outcomes unless the unchanged calculation meets the runtime
screen on a fresh microbenchmark. Do not revive the failed context residual,
imitation, UCT/MCTS, broad self-play fitting, or a budget sweep.
