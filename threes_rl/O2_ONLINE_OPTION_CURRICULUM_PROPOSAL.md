# O2 Proposal: Online Scale-Equivariant Option Curriculum

Date: 2026-07-26

Status: proposal only. No execution, rollout, label, model fit, stream
reservation, policy evaluation, or dashboard change is authorized.

## Why O2

O1 P0 passed its representation and prospective-power checks but sealed
`HOLD_O1_DATA_OR_POWER`: all 3,242 retained replay files belong to protected
forensics, continuation, diagnostic, rank/score, or playlist-selected
namespaces, leaving zero eligible natural roots. Reusing those sources would
weaken the frozen provenance contract. Routine exact-rung acquisition would
repeat the G1/G2 scarcity loop.

O2 keeps the machine-defined relational option thesis but changes the data
mechanism materially: learn options online from complete fresh normal-start
games. Earlier-scale option episodes arise naturally and train a shared
scale-equivariant policy; later scales test upward transfer. No behavior or
human action is ever a label.

## Scientific Question

Can a closed-loop option learner trained online on abundant lower-scale
duplicate-pair transitions improve full-game progression and score when it
controls every move of an invoked option?

## Proposed Contract

1. Preserve O1 A1-A6 geometry, pair provenance, A3 air safety, action-
   conditioned model schema, and machine-only option outcomes.
2. Generate new completed normal-start ancestries under fresh streams. Outside
   an option, use the frozen incumbent. During round 1 options, sample uniformly
   from legal actions; later rounds use one frozen epsilon schedule.
3. Train only on simulator-generated option success, time-to-stage, and
   successor-geometry distributions. Do not use recorded actions, terminal
   game score, or human choices as targets.
4. Use targets 48/96/192/384 for fitting. Treat 768 as development transfer and
   1536 as sealed transfer/confirmation; never upweight a favorable ancestry.
5. Keep all option episodes from one complete game in one partition and weight
   whole ancestries equally for evaluation.
6. Freeze one architecture, optimizer, loss, rollout count, stream namespace,
   and checkpoint before generation. No sweep or post-outcome reward change.
7. Evaluate mechanism with full closed-loop option exposure on fresh paired
   normal-start games, not a one-move continuation gate. Primary endpoints are
   paired score-minus-starter and next-scale transition hazard; survival, air,
   anchor integrity, lower tail, P1536, and P3072 are safeguards.
8. A development pass may open one sealed normal-start confirmation. Only
   confirmation may change the incumbent or dashboard.

## Outcome-Free Readiness Gate

Before execution, freeze:

- a small equal-family normal-start source-yield pilot and a hard game/runtime
  cap;
- expected option episodes per complete ancestry at each fitting scale;
- an ancestry-level power/MDE design for the full-policy paired endpoint;
- exact family and stream identities, service/disk limits, and marker-first
  orchestration;
- stop rules for inadequate option activity, family concentration, transfer
  scarcity, runtime, or storage.

The pilot may inspect only completion, integrity, and machine-defined option
eligibility counts. It may not inspect scores, actions, milestones after a
selected root, or policy outcomes.

## Ranked Course Options

1. **O2 online option curriculum**: best match to the diagnosed data bottleneck
   while preserving closed-loop, scale-transfer learning.
2. **Joint policy/value/search self-play**: materially broader and more
   expensive; only appropriate if O2 cannot generate stable lower-to-upper
   option transfer.
3. **Human board-scenario training ground**: remains last resort and query-only
   unless simulator rollouts later establish causal action advantage.

O1 is held for unavailable clean retained data, not killed as a
representation. Exact depth 3 remains permanently killed. `PROMOTE=false`.
